# ISOMTRIC - Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 29, 2026

## Data We Collect

ISOMTRIC does not collect any personal data on our servers.

All data you enter — workouts, sets, reps, weights, body weight, personal records, and settings — is stored locally on your device and synced privately across your Apple devices via your own iCloud account. We never see it.

## iCloud Sync

Your training data syncs across your devices using Apple iCloud KV storage. This is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy). We have no access to your iCloud data.

## Widgets

ISOMTRIC includes home screen and lock screen widgets. Widget data (streak, Muscle IQ, last workout, weekly volume, PRs, etc.) is read directly from your on-device storage and displayed locally. No data is sent to any server to power the widgets.

## Third Parties

ISOMTRIC does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or any third-party tracking. No data is shared with third parties.

## No Account Required

ISOMTRIC requires no account, email, or sign-in of any kind.

## Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time from the Settings screen inside the app. Deleting the app removes all local data from your device.
